How it works

  1. Create your LumiVisuals account (email + password).
  2. Download the launcher setup and login — if you're already logged in here, just approve the launcher login.
  3. Choose Minimal or Classic, press Launch. The launcher checks Minecraft 1.21.11, installs Fabric, puts the right LumiVisuals mod in place, and starts the game with your profile name.
  4. Your playtime, name and avatar sync between launcher and website.
