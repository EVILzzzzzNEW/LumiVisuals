How it works

  1. Pick a profile name on this page — it's yours instantly, no login.
  2. Download the launcher setup and enter the same name there.
  3. Choose Minimal or Classic, press Launch. The launcher checks Minecraft 1.21.11, installs Fabric, puts the right LumiVisuals mod in place, and starts the game with your profile name.
  4. Your playtime ticks up here and in the launcher, Steam-style.
